01 / About

Engineering ceramic systems beyond conventional geometry.

Siyao Chen is a Postdoctoral Researcher at City University of Hong Kong. His work connects photopolymerization, shape-memory polymer chemistry and pyrolysis to make lightweight, geometrically complex and functional ceramic structures.

His research has examined shape-reconfigurable ceramic precursors, self-deployable structures, and semiconductive polymer-derived ceramics for sensing applications.

02 / Research

Three connected research directions

01

Reconfigurable ceramic precursors

Designing printable preceramic polymers with reconfigurability and shape-memory behaviour, so that a precursor can be programmed before conversion into a complex ceramic part.

  • 4D printing
  • Shape-memory precursors
  • Pyrolytic conversion

02

Functional polymer-derived ceramics

Using vat photopolymerization to manufacture SiCN and SiBCN ceramics, and relating composition and free-carbon evolution to electrical and piezoresistive response.

  • Digital light processing
  • Semiconductive SiCN
  • Ceramic sensors

03

Responsive structures & devices

Combining printed materials, conductive pathways and mechanics to develop deployable structures and intelligent-material concepts for sensing and actuation.

  • Joule-heated actuation
  • Multimaterial systems
  • Mechanics modelling

Research highlight

“

Reconfiguration before pyrolysis offers a route to ceramic architectures that are difficult to form by conventional processing.

Representative work combines a shape-memory, printable preceramic precursor with programmable shape recovery, then converts the programmed structure to lightweight ceramic components.
